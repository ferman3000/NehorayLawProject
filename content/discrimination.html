PROTECTED CLASSES

California law protects individuals from illegal discrimination in employment based on the following:

  • Age (40 and over)
  • Ancestry
  • Color
  • Creed
  • Denial of Family and Medical Care Leave
  • Denial of Pregnancy Disability Leave or Reasonable Accommodation
  • Disability (mental and physical) including HIV and AIDS
  • Marital Status
  • Medical Condition (cancer and genetic characteristics)
  • National Origin
  • Race
  • Religion
  • Sex
  • Sexual Orientation

YOUR RIGHTS & REMEDIES

You have the right to be free from discrimination in the workplace which is based on any of these protected characteristics, and to receive compensation for any damages such unlawful conduct causes you.
